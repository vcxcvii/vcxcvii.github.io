{% comment %} The masthead. One full-bleed sticky bar carrying the mark, the wayfinding links, the contact action and the page light. It sits outside #wrapper in _layouts/default.html. A bar cannot span the window from inside a 46rem centred column, and navigation was never main content anyway. Its inner div re-establishes that column so the mark lines up with the first character of every heading below it. data/navigation.yml drives both poles. An item marked `pole: right` leaves the wayfinding group and becomes the action at the far end of the bar, which is where a reader looks for the one thing the site wants them to do. {% endcomment %}
