days since this site went live, on 17 May 2026. Borrowed from days.ramp.com, which counts the days since Ramp was founded so nobody forgets that tomorrow is already too late. Here is why I built mine.

Every week of the year appears below, whether or not anything happened in it. Each one can carry a stated intention, and under it the things that landed, dated. Essays and side-quest releases add themselves, because they are already published and dated elsewhere on this site. Everything else I write down by hand. Finished work is struck through, work I did not finish stays open after the week ends, and work I killed says so.
